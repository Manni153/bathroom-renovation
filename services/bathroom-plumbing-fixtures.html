Who This Is For

Situations that usually bring people to this page

A shower valve has failed

Your mixer shower or thermostatic valve is giving inconsistent temperature or pressure, or has stopped working outright, and you need it diagnosed and replaced.

Swapping a bath or basin

You want a freestanding bath instead of a built-in one, or a tired vanity and basin replaced, without redoing the tiling or layout around it.

Weak or uneven pressure

Showers that never quite feel right — often a mains-pressure issue in older Algarve properties, and usually fixable without touching the walls.

On low pressure specifically: many older Algarve properties run their bathroom straight off mains pressure, with no booster pump or accumulator tank anywhere in the system. Mains pressure in parts of the region is genuinely on the modest side, so a mixer shower or thermostatic valve that's working perfectly well can still feel weak or uneven — particularly on an upper floor or at the end of a long supply run. It's a real, checkable regional factor, and it's usually fixable by adding a pump or accumulator tank rather than re-piping the property.

What's Included

What we fit, supply and diagnose

  • Supply and/or fitting of taps, mixer showers and thermostatic valves
  • Supply and/or fitting of basins, WCs, bidets, bathtubs and vanity units
  • Isolating valves and small supply or waste pipework alterations needed to fit new fixtures
  • Pressure diagnostics to identify whether an issue is the fixture or the supply
  • Booster pump or accumulator tank fitting where mains pressure is the cause
  • Removal and disposal of the old fixture being replaced
Standalone Job, Not a Renovation

This is plumbing and fixtures on your existing layout

Everything on this page assumes your bathroom's layout, tiling and waterproofing stay as they are — we're replacing or repairing what's already there, not rebuilding the room. If you're planning new tiling, a layout change, or a full strip-out, that's our full bathroom renovation service, which includes plumbing as part of a bigger coordinated project. And if removing an old fixture uncovers a leak or a failed seal that needs proper tanking, that's covered separately under our waterproofing service — see the FAQ below for how that's handled if it comes up mid-job.
