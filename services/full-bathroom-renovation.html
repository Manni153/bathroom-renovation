Who This Is For

Three situations that usually bring people to us

The bathroom feels dated

1990s or 2000s tiling, an old bathtub nobody uses, poor lighting — you want the whole room to feel current, not another patch repair.

Renovating before selling

An outdated bathroom is one of the first things buyers or valuers notice. A full renovation with neutral, well-finished materials protects your asking price.

Converting for accessibility

Replacing a step-in bath or shower tray with a level-access layout for long-term comfort — see also our wet room installation page for the fully tanked version of this.

What's Included

Everything between strip-out and final fix

  • Strip-out and disposal of the existing suite, tiling and, where needed, flooring
  • First-fix plumbing — supply and waste alterations within the agreed layout
  • Waterproofing (tanking) of shower zones, floors and wet walls to NP EN 14891 practice
  • Floor and wall tiling, including levelling, waterproof substrate prep and grouting
  • Plastering and making good of walls disturbed by the works
  • Second-fix plumbing and fitting of sanitaryware, shower enclosure or bath, vanity and taps
  • Electrical work coordinated with our certified electrician partner — not performed in-house
  • Site protection, daily clean-up, and full debris removal at completion

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Strip-out and disposal of the old suite, first-fix plumbing changes, waterproofing (tanking) of wet zones, floor and wall tiling, second-fix plumbing, and fitting of sanitaryware, shower enclosure or bath, vanity unit and taps. Plastering and making good of walls is included where the renovation exposes or damages them. Electrical work — new circuits, extractor fan wiring, underfloor heating elements — is coordinated with our certified electrician partner and scheduled into the same project timeline.

As a regional guide: a like-for-like renovation (same layout, mid-range fixtures and porcelain tiling) typically runs €5,500–€9,500. A renovation that changes the layout — moving the shower, adding a double vanity, upgrading to large-format or natural stone tiling — runs €9,500–€16,000. A full wet room conversion with structural drainage changes runs €16,000–€26,000+. Villas with underfloor heating, custom joinery or imported fittings run higher. Every job gets a written, itemised quote before work starts — these are guide figures, not a substitute for one.

A like-for-like renovation typically takes 10–14 working days from strip-out to final fix. A renovation with layout changes or a wet room conversion typically takes 14–20 working days, partly because waterproofing membranes need proper curing time before tiling begins — that curing time is not a step we compress to hit a deadline.

A renovation that keeps the same layout, doesn't touch load-bearing walls, and doesn't reroute waste pipes through a shared building generally doesn't need a municipal building licence. If you're in an apartment building (condomínio), it's still good practice to notify the building administration before works involving noise, water shut-offs, or shared risers — we can provide a written scope of works to share with them. If your renovation does involve moving a wall or changing the drainage route, we'll flag it and advise on the comunicação prévia process with the local Câmara Municipal before quoting.

We'll tell you honestly during the site visit whether your layout change is worth the added cost and time, rather than defaulting to the more expensive option.

We use liquid-applied waterproofing membranes compliant with NP EN 14891 beneath ceramic and stone tiling in shower zones, floors, and up to 1.8–2m on walls in direct-wet areas — with particular attention to floor-drain collars, corner joints, and the tile-adhesive compatibility of the system, since a membrane is only as good as its detailing at these junctions.

Portugal applies a reduced 6% VAT rate (instead of the standard 23%) to renovation labour on residential properties that are at least two years old, subject to conditions set out in the Portuguese VAT code. Eligibility depends on the property's age, use and the scope of works — we confirm whether your project qualifies as part of the quoting process rather than assuming either rate applies.
