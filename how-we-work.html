How We Work

A considered process, explained plainly

Here's exactly how a project runs with us, from first enquiry to final sign-off — including payment and how scope changes are handled along the way.

Home / How We Work
The Process

Six steps, start to finish

1

Enquiry

You tell us what you're planning — a full renovation, a shower conversion, a tiling job — and roughly when. We reply within one working day.

2

Site visit or remote survey

We assess the space in person where possible, or by video walkthrough and measurements you supply if you're not in Portugal yet.

3

Written, itemised quote

A fixed price for the agreed scope, materials and timeline — in writing, not a verbal figure. You take the time you need to review it.

4

Scheduling

We confirm a start date, agree access arrangements (including with a keyholder or property manager if you're abroad), and order materials.

5

The work itself

Your renovation carried out to the agreed scope and schedule, by our team, with photo and video updates at each stage.

6

Walkthrough and sign-off

A final inspection with you, in person or by video, before we consider the project complete. Any snag items are agreed and closed out before final payment.

Working With Owners Abroad

If you're not in Portugal for the renovation

Many of our clients agree the entire scope of work before they've set foot on-site for the year. Here's how that works in practice.

  • Survey by video call and photos/measurements you take yourself and send us over WhatsApp, with our guidance on what to capture
  • Quote and scope confirmed by phone or WhatsApp, with questions answered before you approve anything
  • Access coordinated with a keyholder, property manager, or a neighbour, by arrangement with you
  • Progress photos and short videos sent over WhatsApp at each major stage of the renovation
  • Remote walkthrough and sign-off by video call before final payment, or in person if your visit timing allows
Payment & Changes

How payment and scope changes work

Payment terms are set out in writing with your quote — typically a deposit to schedule materials and labour, a stage payment at an agreed milestone, and a final payment on completion. We don't request full payment upfront. If you want to change anything once work is underway — a different tile, an added fixture — we price the change and confirm it in writing before proceeding, so your final invoice never contains a surprise.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Your renovation is delivered by our team as a single accountable business — one point of contact and one written quote for the whole project, from first measure to final sign-off.

Payment structure is confirmed in writing with your quote — typically a deposit to schedule materials and labour, a stage payment at a defined project milestone, and a final payment on completion and sign-off. We don't ask for full payment upfront.

Changes to the agreed scope — a different tile, an added fixture, a layout tweak — are priced and confirmed in writing before we proceed. We don't add cost to your final invoice without agreeing it with you first.

Workmanship is covered under written terms confirmed at quote stage, and materials carry their manufacturer's own warranty. Specific terms depend on the scope of your project and are set out in your quote, not implied generally on this page.
