Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026.

Who we are

Bathrooms Algarve ("we", "us", "our") provides bathroom renovation services to homeowners across the Algarve, Portugal. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Portuguese data protection law.

What we collect

We don't run online enquiry forms — you reach us by phone call or WhatsApp. When you contact us, we collect the information you give us directly: your name, phone number, the town or area of your property, and any project details, photos or measurements you share (for example over WhatsApp). If you call us, we may note down the same details from the conversation. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically logs basic technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and site-performance purposes. This site has no contact or quote-request forms, so no form data is collected through the website itself.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide a quote for renovation work
  • To schedule and coordinate the project you've engaged us for, including sharing relevant contact and site-access details with our certified electrician partner where electrical work is part of your project
  • To contact you about your enquiry by phone or WhatsApp
  • To maintain records required for invoicing, warranty and legal/tax compliance

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for purposes unrelated to responding to your enquiry or delivering a project you've engaged us for.

Who we share it with

WhatsApp messages are handled through WhatsApp's own service (operated by Meta) — their privacy policy governs how a message is transmitted and stored on their platform. Where your project includes electrical work, we share the minimum necessary contact and site-access details with our certified electrician partner so they can complete and sign off that part of the job. We don't share your information with any other third party for marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you and, if you become a client, for the duration of the project plus the period required by Portuguese tax and accounting law (generally ten years for invoicing records). Enquiries that don't proceed to a project are deleted or anonymised after 24 months.

Cookies

This site uses only essential cookies required for basic functionality. If we add analytics or marketing cookies in future, we will update this policy and request consent where required by law.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and the right to data portability and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us by phone or WhatsApp at +351 289 100 200. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with Portugal's data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD).

Contact

Questions about this policy or how your data is handled: call or WhatsApp us at +351 289 100 200.
