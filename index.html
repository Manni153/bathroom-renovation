Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Most bathroom renovations are exempt from a full building licence (licença de obras) when the work stays inside the existing footprint and doesn't touch load-bearing walls, the building's exterior, or the drainage stack routing. Moving a wall, changing the waste pipe route through a shared building, or altering a façade-facing window typically requires comunicação prévia or a licence from the local Câmara Municipal. We review your layout before quoting and tell you plainly whether your project needs paperwork — we don't guess or skip this step.

A straightforward full renovation typically runs 10–18 working days once materials are on site, covering everything from strip-out to final finishing. Wet rooms and layout changes usually add 3–6 days. We give you a written day-by-day schedule before work starts, not a vague estimate.

We take on bathroom renovation projects across the Algarve, including Faro, Albufeira, Lagos, Portimão, Vilamoura, Tavira, Loulé, Silves, Carvoeiro, Quinta do Lago, Vale do Lobo, Olhão, São Brás de Alportel, Aljezur, Quarteira and Almancil, plus the villages and urbanisations in between.
