Lagos Properties

Two very different building stocks, one town

Lagos draws a lot of British, Irish and Dutch buyers who want its beaches, surf culture and old-town character — a place that feels more like a real working town than the larger resort centres. That shows up in the bathrooms we're asked to renovate here, which tend to fall into two quite different categories.

Within that buyer mix, the decision-making tends to split fairly cleanly along the two property types below. Owners settling into old-town townhouses are more often moving to Lagos full-time or spending the majority of the year here, and they're usually motivated by the town itself — walking distance to the marina and old town, the daily rhythm of a place that still functions as a working town outside peak season — rather than by rental income. Owners of the newer villas and apartments outside the walls are more of a mix: some are full-time residents who wanted more space and a driveway, others split their time between Lagos and a home country and let the property for part of the year. Even where a property is let out seasonally, the renovation brief is usually still driven by how the owners themselves want to use the space when they're in residence, rather than by a rental-yield calculation — which shapes the requests we get toward comfort and finish quality rather than the fastest or cheapest fix.

Historic centre townhouses

Traditional townhouses in and around the old walled town, often older masonry construction. Bathrooms here can have plumbing and waterproofing that predates any modern standard entirely — sometimes tiled straight onto masonry with no membrane underneath. Surveying properly before quoting matters more here than almost anywhere else we work.

Villas & apartments outside the walls

Newer builds around Meia Praia, Porto de Mós and along the Lagos–Sagres road corridor, popular with buyers wanting more space and a driveway. Many owners are relocating full-time or splitting their time between Lagos and home, and the requests here lean toward comfort upgrades — a walk-in shower replacing an old bath — over anything done for resale.

A Genuine Local Constraint

Access in the old town affects scheduling, not just cost

The historic centre's narrow cobbled streets are a real practical factor on a renovation project, not a footnote. Skip placement and material deliveries often need to work around limited or no vehicle access — full-size trucks and skip lorries simply can't reach some streets. Depending on your address, that can mean a smaller skip, a fixed delivery window, or walking materials in by hand from the nearest point a vehicle can park. We'd rather tell you this plainly at the site visit and plan around it than discover it mid-project and let it blow the schedule.

In practice, a job inside the old walls and a job in Meia Praia or Porto de Mós run to noticeably different rhythms. On an old-town street, deliveries are usually staged in smaller loads timed around whatever loading window the street allows, waste is bagged and carried out rather than skipped at the kerb, and the crew works around whatever access the property itself gives them — some townhouses open straight onto the street, others involve carrying materials and spoil through a narrow hallway or stairwell. On the newer developments outside the walls, a skip usually sits on the drive or verge for the duration of the job and deliveries arrive in the ordinary way, so the schedule there is driven far more by what's found once the old bathroom is stripped out than by how materials get to the door. Either way, we agree the realistic access plan for your specific street or plot at the site visit, before it's built into the written quote.

Services For Lagos Properties

Where we'd start, depending on your property

Waterproofing

The starting point for most old-town townhouse bathrooms — bringing a wet zone up to a proper NP EN 14891 membrane standard before any tiling goes back on, especially where the existing setup predates that standard entirely. Because so much of the historic centre's building stock has never been waterproofed to any formal standard at all, this is often the job that determines whether a townhouse bathroom can be safely retiled, not a cosmetic add-on to a wider renovation.

Full Bathroom Renovation

For older centre properties where the layout and finish all need attention together — a complete renovation delivered as one project rather than a string of separate repairs. It also suits owners settling into Lagos full-time who want the whole room brought up to a consistent standard in one planned project, rather than returning to patch it again in a year or two.

Walk-In Shower Conversion

The most requested upgrade among owners settling into Meia Praia, Porto de Mós and the villas along the Sagres corridor — swapping an old bath for a level-access shower, chosen for day-to-day comfort rather than rental yield. Because these properties typically already have modern plumbing and a waterproofed wet zone in reasonable condition, the job is usually a contained one focused on the shower footprint itself, which keeps both cost and disruption lower than the equivalent job in an old-town townhouse.

We treat this as the norm rather than the exception when quoting: the site visit looks closely for damp, hidden leaks and structural moisture before we scope the job, and any wet zone we open up gets a liquid-applied waterproofing membrane to NP EN 14891 as standard, not as an optional extra.

A renovation that keeps the existing layout, doesn't touch load-bearing walls, and doesn't reroute waste pipes through a shared building generally doesn't need a municipal building licence. Some buildings in and around the historic walled centre sit within areas with added conservation sensitivity, so if your renovation involves any external work or structural change we'll flag whether comunicação prévia to the Câmara Municipal de Lagos applies before quoting. If you're in an apartment building, it's good practice to notify the condomínio administration ahead of any works involving noise or shared risers — we can provide a written scope of works for that purpose.
