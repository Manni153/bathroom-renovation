Areas We Cover

Bathroom renovation across the Algarve

From the western capes to the Spanish border, and inland to the villages behind the coast — sixteen towns, one accountable team.

Home / Areas We Cover

Faro

The Algarve's capital — a working city, not a resort, with a large stock of older apartment blocks due an update.

Albufeira

The Algarve's busiest resort town, with a huge stock of holiday-let apartments and villas.

Lagos

A historic walled town in the west Algarve, popular with British, Irish and Dutch buyers.

Portimão

A regional hub with the high-rise Praia da Rocha seafront and an affordable town centre.

Vilamoura

A purpose-built marina and golf resort with a formal condominium-managed property stock.

Tavira

A quieter, historic riverside town near the Spanish border, popular for its understated character.

Loulé

A traditional Algarve market town with an affordable, mostly year-round resident property market.

Silves

A historic inland town surrounded by countryside quintas, some on private well water.

Carvoeiro

A small clifftop former fishing village, popular for compact holiday apartments and villas.

Quinta do Lago

An exclusive golf resort of large architect-designed luxury villas bordering the Ria Formosa.

Vale do Lobo

A long-established luxury resort with a wider mix of villa ages than its Quinta do Lago neighbour.

Olhão

A working fishing town on the Ria Formosa, known for traditional flat-roofed Algarvian houses.

São Brás de Alportel

A small inland hill town with a quiet, village pace and countryside smallholdings nearby.

Aljezur

A rural west-coast surf town, popular for characterful cottages on well and septic systems.

Quarteira

An older, more affordable seafront town next to Vilamoura with a large year-round population.

Almancil

The service hub for the surrounding luxury resorts, with its own ordinary town centre too.

Don't see your town listed?

We take on projects across the wider Algarve, including the villages and urbanisations between these towns.
