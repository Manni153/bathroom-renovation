Albufeira's Property Mix

Three types of Albufeira bathroom we work on most

Holiday-let apartments

Albufeira has an enormous stock of apartments used partly or fully as short-term rentals. Ensuites in 1980s–2000s complexes are the most common job: dated tiling and fixtures that need to look fresh and hold up to repeat guest turnover, not just look good on day one.

Villas in Branqueira & Olhos de Água

Larger-scale renovations and ensuite or wet room conversions, often across multiple bathrooms in one project, for owners upgrading a personal or rental villa.

Old Town & Marina properties

Older buildings around Albufeira's clifftop Old Town and the Marina area, where bathroom renovations are usually paired with a proper waterproofing check given the age of the building fabric.

Working Around the Rental Calendar

Off-season scheduling, with a finish date you can list against

A large share of Albufeira owners are UK and Irish, and many run the property as a rental income asset alongside personal use — which means the renovation timeline has to work around the letting calendar, not the other way round. Most owners want work done in the off-season, roughly November through February, to avoid losing peak-season bookings. We plan to that window and give you a firm finish date you can hold a re-listing date against, rather than an open-ended "we'll see."

Built for Turnover

Finishes chosen for guest wear, not just first impressions

  • Porcelain tiling specified for durability under weekly guest turnover, not just first-fix appearance
  • Low-maintenance fixtures that hold up to repeat use with minimal upkeep between changeovers
  • Waterproofing re-checked and re-tanked to NP EN 14891 practice on older complexes, where the original membrane is often the real issue hiding under tired-looking tiling
  • Condominium-aware scheduling for resort complexes — written scope of works you can share with building administration
  • Fast plumbing and fixture turnarounds for contained jobs between bookings

For holiday-let bathrooms we generally recommend porcelain tiling over natural stone or highly polished finishes — it's harder-wearing, easier to keep looking presentable between cleans, and more forgiving of the wear that comes with weekly guest changeovers. Fixtures are specified for durability and low maintenance rather than for the lowest purchase price, since a fixture that fails mid-season costs more in disruption than it saves upfront.

Many of Albufeira's holiday-let apartments sit inside condominium-managed resort complexes, and it's good practice to notify the building administration before works involving noise, water shut-offs, or shared risers — we provide a written scope of works you can pass on. A renovation that keeps the same layout and doesn't reroute waste pipes through shared building infrastructure generally doesn't need a separate municipal building licence; if your plans do involve moving a wall or changing the drainage route, we'll flag that and advise on the comunicação prévia process with the Câmara Municipal before quoting.

A lot of Albufeira's apartment stock dates to this period, and the most common issue we find at strip-out is waterproofing that has degraded well beyond what's visible on the surface — grout that looks fine but a membrane underneath that's failed, particularly around shower trays and floor drains. We inspect the substrate as soon as the old tiling is off and re-tank to NP EN 14891 standard before any new tiling goes down, rather than tiling over a compromised membrane to save a day.

For contained jobs — a failing shower mixer, a leaking WC, a tired vanity and taps — yes, this is a large part of what we do in Albufeira between guest bookings. As a regional guide, the same ranges apply as elsewhere in the Algarve: a like-for-like renovation with porcelain tiling and mid-range, durable fixtures typically runs €5,500–€9,500, and a renovation that changes the layout or upgrades finishes runs €9,500–€16,000. Villas in areas like Branqueira and Olhos de Água with larger bathrooms or ensuite counts run higher. For rental properties we always confirm the off-season working window as part of the quote, since that governs the schedule as much as the budget does.
