Vale do Lobo

Bathroom renovation for Vale do Lobo's villas, townhouses and praça apartments

Vale do Lobo's clifftop setting above its two beaches and long-established golf courses comes with one of the widest mixes of property age on this stretch of coast — well-established luxury villas from the resort's earlier development decades sit alongside newer builds and the upscale apartments and townhouses around the praça. We scope each renovation to the property it's actually in, and register works with the resort's estate management alongside any municipal requirement.

Vale do Lobo's Properties

Three renovation starting points, not one

Established luxury villas

Villas from the resort's earlier development decades, often clifftop or close to the beaches. Bathrooms here are typically due a full modern update — layout, waterproofing, tiling and fixtures — rather than a cosmetic refresh.

Newer villa builds

More recently built villas, closer in spirit to Quinta do Lago's architect-led stock next door. Renovations here are more often about finish upgrades — tiling, vanities, fittings — on plumbing and waterproofing that's already sound.

Praça apartments & townhouses

A significant stock of upscale apartments and townhouses around Vale do Lobo's praça, generally newer or recently updated, with condominium-style building administration alongside the resort's own management.

Who We Work With

Owners who visit, not owners who live on site

Many Vale do Lobo owners are internationally based and visit seasonally rather than year-round, so a renovation usually needs to be planned, quoted, carried out and signed off without requiring you to be physically present for most of it. That shapes how we run the project as much as the property itself does: clear written scope agreed remotely, photo updates through the works, and coordination with your property manager or a local keyholder where one is in place.

Local Considerations
  • Renovation work is generally expected to be registered with Vale do Lobo's resort estate management ahead of any municipal requirement that applies to the works themselves.
  • Villas from the resort's earlier development decades often have first-generation tiling laid without a proper waterproofing membrane, and original plumbing runs worth assessing during strip-out.
  • Praça apartments and townhouses typically involve a building administration alongside the resort's own access and contractor policies — we provide a written scope of works for both.
  • Seasonal, internationally based ownership means projects are usually planned to run while owners are away, with remote sign-off rather than requiring you on site.

We handle that registration as part of scheduling your project: submitting a written scope of works, confirming permitted access windows, and coordinating with the resort's management ahead of the start date, so it isn't something you have to chase from abroad.

Vale do Lobo has a wider spread of property ages than some neighbouring resorts, and a bathroom in an original-era villa often still has its first-generation suite: dated tiling laid without a proper waterproofing membrane underneath, original plumbing runs, and a layout that predates today's walk-in shower and double-vanity expectations. None of that is unusual for a villa of that generation. We treat these as full renovations by default — checking supply and waste condition during strip-out, waterproofing to current standard before retiling, and updating the layout where you want it — rather than a cosmetic refresh over an ageing substrate.

As a guide for Vale do Lobo's villa and apartment stock: a like-for-like renovation in a newer villa or praça apartment (same layout, quality fixtures and porcelain or stone-look tiling) typically runs €7,000–€12,000. A full renovation of an older-era villa bathroom — new layout, waterproofing, tiling and fixtures — typically runs €12,000–€19,000. A wet room conversion with structural drainage changes, more common as a full renovation choice for these villas, typically runs €18,000–€28,000+. Natural stone tiling, underfloor heating and imported fittings run higher. Every job gets a written, itemised quote after a site visit.

Most of the owners we work with in Vale do Lobo are internationally based and visit seasonally, so we plan the project to run start-to-finish while you're away rather than requiring you on site. That means an initial video or in-person site visit and a written, itemised quote agreed remotely, progress updates with photos through the works, and final sign-off — either in person on your next visit or remotely if you prefer — before we consider the job complete. Access and delivery windows are coordinated directly with the resort's property management and, where relevant, a local keyholder or your own property manager.

The building administration of your block, alongside the resort's own estate management, usually needs to be notified before works involving noise, water shut-offs, or any shared riser — similar to condomínio rules elsewhere in the Algarve, just layered on top of the resort's own access and contractor policies. We provide a written scope of works you or your property manager can pass to both. Praça apartments and townhouses are more often newer or recently updated than the resort's original villa stock, so renovations here more commonly focus on finish upgrades — tiling, fixtures, vanities — than full waterproofing rebuilds, though we assess each on its own condition rather than assuming.

We use liquid-applied waterproofing membranes compliant with NP EN 14891 beneath ceramic and stone tiling in shower zones, floors, and up walls in direct-wet areas, with particular attention to floor-drain collars and corner detailing. It matters more in villas from the resort's earlier development decades, where a bathroom retiled at some point since may have gone straight over the original surface with no membrane underneath — a common, hidden cause of damp that only shows up as staining or a soft floor years later. We check for this during strip-out rather than assuming a previous retile was done to current standard.
