What We Do

Bathroom renovation, full scope

Bathrooms Algarve handles the full scope of a bathroom renovation project: layout planning, plumbing, waterproofing and tanking, tiling, plastering, and fitting sanitaryware and fixtures. We work as a single accountable team, so you have one point of contact and one written quote covering the whole job — not separate trades to schedule and chase yourself.

Electrical work — extractor fans, lighting circuits, underfloor heating wiring — is coordinated with a certified electrician partner as part of the same project timeline. That work is carried out and signed off by the electrician partner directly; we don't perform electrical installation in-house, and we don't imply otherwise.

Who We Work With

English-speaking homeowners across the Algarve

Most of our clients are UK, Irish, Dutch and German owners of apartments and villas across the Algarve — some living here full-time, many managing a renovation from abroad around visits or through a property manager. That shapes how we work: written quotes and clear scope documents rather than verbal agreements, photo and video progress updates for owners who can't be on-site, and plain answers to the practical questions that come up around Portuguese permits, condominium rules and building standards.

Standards We Work To

What "done properly" means to us

  • Waterproofing beneath tiling follows NP EN 14891 liquid-membrane practice in wet zones, with attention to the junctions and floor-drain details where membranes typically fail
  • Every project gets a written, itemised quote before work starts — not a verbal estimate
  • Scope changes are priced and agreed in writing before we proceed, never added silently to a final invoice
  • We tell you plainly when a project needs a comunicação prévia or municipal permit, rather than leaving you to find out later
  • Electrical work is coordinated with, and carried out by, a certified electrician partner — never implied as in-house work it isn't
